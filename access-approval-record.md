# Ironbridge Bank & Trust | Access Approval Record: Two Rivers Onboarding

## Overview

Following the acquisition of Two Rivers Savings, fourteen new hires were provisioned. Access was justified in three steps. The official HR onboarding roster was the authoritative source for who these fourteen people are. HR assigned each person a job title, and each title is tied to a matching role group that automatically grants the software and access needed. High-risk permissions and administrative power are not part of that automatic grant and require manager approval.

## Authoritative Source

The authoritative source was the official HR onboarding roster. Account creation begins with official HR records to verify that only verified hired employees get the access needed.

## How Access Was Decided

HR assigns each person a job title. The title is tied to a matching role group, which automatically gives the necessary software and access needed.

## Access Granted and Justification

| Access | Approval Basis | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| Standard software, email, and basic tools | Pre-approved by role definition | The person's HR job title is tied to a matching role group, which automatically gives the necessary software and access needed. |
| High-risk permissions and administrative power | Requires manager approval | Not pre-approved by role definition. Each grant needs individual approval. |
| Wire operations hire: basic daily tools | Granted by role | The role granted the basic daily tools. Wire transfer access was withheld (see Exception below). |

## Exception: Wire Operations Hire

His role granted him the basic daily tools, but withheld wire transfer access.

Normally, the Head of Wire Operations must approve it after a background check and business review.

IT cannot grant it because security roles require separation of duties and a formal approval process.
